Privacy

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Short version: Paralingo is a local app. Your audio is never uploaded. Your transcripts are never seen. Your translations happen on-device.

Last updated · April 22, 2026
TL;DR: Every stage of the dictate → translate → paste flow runs on your Mac. Voice capture, WhisperKit transcription, Apple Translation, and clipboard paste are all local operations. Paralingo's capture pipeline makes zero network requests. The only network activity is an optional crash report and an optional update check, both of which you can disable.

What we collect

Nothing about your voice or your text. Audio recordings, transcripts, translations, and history entries live on your Mac, in the folder Paralingo manages (~/Library/Application Support/Paralingo/).

Crash reports (opt-in, disabled in v0.1)

Paralingo v0.1 ships with no crash-reporting SDK at all. When we later add one, it will be opt-in, carry only the stack trace + device model + app version, and never include any audio, transcript, translation, or clipboard content. We'll update this policy and note the change in the changelog before the toggle exists.

Software updates

On launch, Paralingo checks a single URL for the latest release version. No data is sent beyond the HTTP headers your system normally attaches. You can disable the check in Settings → General.

What we don't collect

  • Your voice recordings, ever
  • Your transcripts, in any language
  • Your translated text
  • Your clipboard contents
  • Which apps you paste into
  • Which languages you translate between
  • Your hotkey configuration
  • Usage analytics, click-tracking, or telemetry

How the pipeline works under the hood

Paralingo is a thin coordinator around Apple's on-device ML stack:

  • Audio capture — AVAudioEngine reads your microphone into a local buffer. Audio is written to a temporary WAV on disk and deleted after transcription.
  • Transcription — WhisperKit runs OpenAI's Whisper Large v3 Turbo on the Apple Neural Engine. The model is downloaded once to ~/Documents/huggingface/ and then runs offline. No audio leaves your Mac.
  • Translation — Apple Translation provides neural translation between 20 target languages. The first time you pick a language pair, macOS downloads a ~60 MB model; after that, translation runs entirely locally.
  • Text cleanup — optional smart capitalization, punctuation, and filler-word trimming. Pure string transforms, no network.
  • Paste — synthesizes a ⌘V keystroke into the focused app via CGEvent. Requires macOS Accessibility permission.

None of these APIs make outbound network calls for your content. Run Little Snitch or tcpdump with Paralingo running — the dictation path is silent.

Permissions

macOS requires your explicit consent for everything below. Each is only requested the first time the relevant feature is used:

  • Microphone — needed the moment you first hold the hotkey to dictate. Used only while the key is held.
  • Accessibility — needed for two things: listening to global hotkeys (Paralingo doesn't steal focus to detect them) and synthesizing the paste keystroke.
  • Screen Recording — not required.
  • Contacts / Calendar / etc. — not required.

Your data, your control

Everything Paralingo saves is in one folder, in plain files you can inspect. Delete it, back it up, let Spotlight index it — it's yours. Uninstalling Paralingo leaves your history behind because it was never ours to take.

Children

Paralingo isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone because we don't collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we ever do collect something new — even anonymously — we'll update this page and note the change in the changelog. The "Last updated" date above will move forward, and the change will be called out in the release notes of the build that introduced it.
