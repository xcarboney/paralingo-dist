How it works

Three steps, zero friction.

No app to switch to. No tab to open. Just speak and paste.

1

Pick your pair

Choose the language you speak and the one you want to paste — any of 20 + auto-detect. Change it from the menu bar any time.

2

Hold and speak

Hold your hotkey (default ⌥), say what you want in your own language, release.

3

Translated text pastes

Paralingo transcribes with WhisperKit, translates with Apple's on-device models, and pastes the result at your cursor.

Features

Everything you'd expect. Nothing you wouldn't.

Small app, sharp focus. Every feature earns its place in the speak-and-paste loop.

WhisperKit transcription

Large-v3 Turbo Whisper model runs on the Neural Engine. 99 input languages, accurate under accents, fast under 300ms.

Apple Translation

On-device translation through Apple's own neural models. 20 target languages, instant once downloaded, no accounts, no keys.

Auto-detect source

WhisperKit tells Paralingo what language you spoke — no manual switching. When you speak the target language already, Paralingo skips the round-trip and pastes as-is.

Custom global hotkey

Default is ⌥ (left Option). Remap to any modifier or combo that doesn't clash with other apps — single-key or combo.

Smart cleanup

Filler-word removal, auto-punctuation, smart capitalization — applied before translation so the target text reads natural, not transcript-y.

Searchable history

Every dictation is saved locally: original transcript + translated output + where you pasted it. Search, copy, re-paste any entry.

Private by default

Nothing leaves your Mac. No account. No API keys. No network calls in the dictation path. Works fully offline once language packs are downloaded.

Live waveform overlay

Subtle on-screen overlay while you're holding the hotkey. Know it's listening, see audio clipping if your mic's too hot.

Per-app language rules

WeChat in Chinese, WhatsApp in Spanish, Slack in English. Set a target language per app in Settings, Paralingo uses it automatically when that app is focused.

Voice-only mode

One tap in the menu bar switches between Translate and Voice-only. Skip translation entirely when you just want pure dictation — same speed, same privacy.

One-tap swap

Flip source and target languages from the menu bar with a single click. Dictating in Spanish after answering in English? Swap and keep going — no settings detour.

Frequently asked

Answers before you ask.

Does Paralingo work on Intel Macs?

No — Apple Silicon only (M1 or later). WhisperKit relies on the Neural Engine and Apple Translation requires a recent macOS. We also require macOS 26 or later because the programmatic TranslationSession API is 26+.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

Never. WhisperKit runs locally, Apple Translation runs locally. There's no network code in the dictation path. You can verify with tcpdump or Little Snitch — Paralingo sits silent during dictation.

How does the translation happen?

After WhisperKit transcribes your audio into text, Paralingo hands that text to Apple's Translation framework (TranslationSession.translate(_:)) targeting your chosen language. The first time you use a language pair, macOS downloads a ~60 MB model. After that, translation is instant and offline.

Which languages are supported?

20 targets (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional). Input coverage is broader — WhisperKit understands 99 languages, so you can speak in almost anything.

Can I use my own hotkey?

Yes. Default is ⌥ (left Option) for hold-to-talk. Remap from Settings → Hotkey to any modifier, letter, number, F-key, or combo.

What happens if I speak the target language already?

Auto-detect kicks in. If Paralingo sees you spoke the same language you're pasting into, the transcript goes through untouched (this is the "Skip when languages match" toggle, on by default).

Where's my transcription history stored?

~/Library/Application Support/Paralingo/. It's a SwiftData container on your disk. Search, copy, or delete entries from Settings → History. Uninstalling removes the app; the history file is yours to keep or trash.
